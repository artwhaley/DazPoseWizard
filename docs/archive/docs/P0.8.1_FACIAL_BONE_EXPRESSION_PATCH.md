# P0.8.1 — Preserve Facial Bone Channels in Expressions

## Purpose

Correct the P0.8 Expression pipeline so an imported Expression preserves authored facial articulation carried by Genesis 8 facial bones as well as authored blendshapes.

This patch supersedes the following P0.8 rules:

- A21: `PerformerExpression` may contain generated Transform channel descriptors.
- A22: clip/wrapper parity covers both blendshape and facial Transform curves.
- A23: Expression sanitization is sparse facial-channel sanitization, not morph-only sanitization.
- A28: an Expression is empty only when no usable blendshape or facial-bone channel remains.
- A29: reports distinguish retained facial skeletal channels from ignored nonfacial skeletal channels.
- A33/A36/A37 and the P0.8A gate: replace the “zero Transform curves” requirement with the ownership and parity rules below.
- B3/B6/B9/B15/B17/B23/B25: the runtime Expression layer may own only the generated facial-bone descriptors and must compose them over the live incoming pose.

All other P0.8 requirements remain in force.

## Product rule

An Expression product contains the union of:

1. active source morph controls, excluding explicitly reserved runtime-owned morphs; and
2. active supported skeletal channels whose target bone belongs to the proven Genesis 8 facial articulation region, excluding bones owned by another performer subsystem.

Dropping a preset into UNITY EXPRESSIONS still declares product intent. It does not make every skeletal channel in that preset an Expression channel.

## Facial-bone region

Determine facial membership from the canonical DAZ figure hierarchy and exact DAZ bone IDs. Do not infer it from labels, substrings, Unity Transform names, or spatial position.

For Genesis 8 Female, a skeletal target is eligible when its DAZ bone ID is:

- `upperFaceRig`, or a descendant of `upperFaceRig`; or
- `lowerJaw`, or a descendant of `lowerJaw`.

This includes the authored upper-face and lower-face articulation trees: brows, eyelids, cheeks, nose, lips, jaw, lower teeth, and tongue when those bones are explicitly driven by the source preset.

The following remain outside Expression ownership:

- `head`, neck, and all torso/limb bones;
- `lEye` and `rEye`, which remain owned by gaze;
- ears and `upperJaw`, which are outside the two proven facial articulation trees;
- any skeletal target that cannot be proven to belong to one of the two trees.

Keep an explicit defensive rejection for `head`, `lEye`, and `rEye` even though the topology rule excludes them. A future hierarchy or mapping error must not allow an Expression to take ownership of gaze bones.

This is a topology rule over stable source IDs. It is not a broad facial-name heuristic.

## Source-channel selection

Start from supported skeletal channels explicitly present in the source preset.

- Retain an eligible bone only when the preset contains at least one materially nonneutral supported channel for that bone.
- Rotation ownership is per bone-local rotation. If any rotation axis is actively driven, emit the evaluated target local quaternion and own the complete local rotation property.
- Translation ownership is per bone-local position. If any translation axis is actively driven, emit the evaluated target local position and own the complete local position property.
- Do not emit inherited motion for descendants that have no active source channel of their own.
- Do not emit a facial bone merely because it exists in the figure or moved in world space due to an animated ancestor.
- Continue ignoring nonfacial skeletal channels, but report each ignored target and the reason.

The current canonical pipeline supports rotation and translation. Nonneutral facial scale channels must not be silently accepted as preserved. Either add end-to-end scale support in the same change or fail the Expression conversion with a clear unsupported-channel diagnostic. Scale support is not required for this patch.

If a preset contains active skeletal channels but the expected facial anchors cannot be found uniquely in the canonical figure hierarchy, fail the Expression conversion. Do not silently produce a morph-only approximation of a bone-authored expression.

## Canonical and resolver changes

Preserve the existing canonical bone IDs, parent IDs, source pose channels, evaluated transforms, and rest transforms. No new filename-based classification is required.

Replace the Expression resolver’s current empty skeletal target set with two sets:

- retained facial target IDs, selected by the topology and active-channel rules above;
- ignored skeletal target IDs with a diagnostic reason.

Run the existing exact bone resolution, rest calibration, evaluation, and original-instance mapping for retained facial targets. A retained target that is missing or ambiguous is a conversion failure. An ignored nonfacial target does not need Unity resolution.

Pose resolution and Pose generation remain unchanged.

## `PerformerExpression` metadata

Keep the existing generated blendshape channel metadata for asset compatibility. Add a generated facial-bone channel collection. Each descriptor contains at least:

- `TransformPath`: exact path under the performer Animator/binding root;
- `DazBoneId`: source identity used for diagnostics and validation;
- `Properties`: flags for local rotation and local position;
- `TargetLocalRotation`: normalized quaternion when rotation is owned;
- `TargetLocalPosition`: vector when position is owned.

No descriptor may target `head`, `lEye`, or `rEye`.

Metadata remains generated by the importer and is not hand-authored. Existing morph-only `PerformerExpression` assets remain valid with an empty facial-bone collection.

## Clip generation and parity

The production `.anim` contains only sanitized Expression-owned curves:

- retained blendshape curves;
- retained facial-bone local rotation curves;
- retained facial-bone local position curves.

It contains no curves for ignored skeletal targets and no other component/property curves.

Enforce exact clip/wrapper parity:

- every blendshape curve has exactly one blendshape descriptor;
- every rotation descriptor has exactly the complete quaternion curve set Unity requires;
- every position descriptor has exactly the complete local-position curve set Unity requires;
- every descriptor references curves present in the clip;
- every curve in the sanitized clip is represented by exactly one descriptor/property flag;
- duplicate paths or duplicate ownership of a property are failures.

Regeneration updates both metadata collections in place and preserves the `.anim` and wrapper GUIDs.

An Expression conversion succeeds when at least one usable blendshape channel or facial-bone property remains. It fails when sanitation leaves neither.

## Runtime composition

The Expression layer remains after Gaze and before Blink:

`BodyPose → base capture → Breathing → Gaze/AttentionLife → Expression → Blink → Animator`

Resolve and cache each facial descriptor to one unique `TransformStreamHandle` when an Expression is requested. Resolve no Transform paths per frame.

For every owned scalar/vector property, preserve the existing live-base semantics. With incoming value `B`, target `T`, and Expression intensity `I`, the settled result is:

`Lerp(B, T, I)`

For local rotation, use a deterministic, shortest-hemisphere normalized quaternion blend. At full intensity the result must equal the imported target quaternion; at zero intensity it must equal the incoming quaternion.

The incoming Transform stream remains live. Pose changes upstream must immediately become the new base for partial Expression intensity. `ClearExpression` removes facial-bone influence and reveals the current incoming Pose-authored local transforms rather than a captured value or rest pose.

### Transition and interruption representation

Represent a transition side as sparse target contributions per owned property, rather than assuming one collapsible scalar target is sufficient for every property type.

For scalar/vector properties, evaluation is the convex combination of the live incoming base and the stored Expression target contributions.

For a rotation property with contribution weights `w[j]`, live incoming quaternion `B`, and target quaternions `T[j]`:

1. hemisphere-align every `T[j]` to `B`;
2. let `W = sum(w[j])`, constrained to `0..1`;
3. evaluate `normalize((1 - W) * B + sum(w[j] * T[j]))`.

A normal settled Expression has one target contribution with weight `I`. During A→B, interpolate the sparse contribution weights using the eased transition progress. On interruption, retain the currently evaluated contribution map as the new source map, then transition it to the new target map. Remove contributions below a small epsilon.

This representation preserves continuity without sampling the final Blink-composited face, keeps the upstream base live, and works for disjoint facial bones as well as blendshapes. Do not approximate interruption by capturing a Transform value from the scene.

## Ownership interactions

- Gaze continues to own `head`, `lEye`, and `rEye`. Expression must neither bind nor write them.
- Expression may own eyelid articulation bones under `upperFaceRig`. Existing autonomous full-blink morph controls remain excluded from Expression assets.
- Blink runs after Expression and must still close from the Expression-modified eyelid state to full closure, then reveal the Expression state on opening.
- Breathing retains its existing bone and morph ownership. The facial topology excludes its skeletal region, and the existing reserved breathing morph exclusions remain.
- SALSA remains out of scope. Jaw, lip, teeth, and tongue bone channels are retained for authored Expressions in P0.8.1. Any future speech ownership policy must compose with or explicitly arbitrate those channels rather than deleting them now.
- Body Pose may contain the same facial-bone properties upstream. Expression overlays the current incoming values sparsely; clearing Expression reveals the Pose-authored values.

## Diagnostics and report

Report at least:

- asset kind;
- source morph controls considered;
- reserved morph controls excluded;
- final blendshape channels emitted;
- active source skeletal properties considered;
- facial skeletal properties retained;
- nonfacial skeletal properties ignored;
- unsupported facial skeletal properties rejected;
- for every retained bone property: DAZ bone ID, Transform path, owned property flags, and target values;
- for every ignored skeletal property: DAZ bone ID, source URL/property, and exclusion reason;
- final blendshape, rotation, position, and total curve counts.

The phrase “source skeletal channels ignored” is insufficient after this patch because some skeletal channels are intentionally retained.

## Desktop verification

Expression output verification must accept the sanitized Transform curves described above and reject all others. It must validate:

- exact `PerformerExpression` script type and clip GUID;
- nonempty combined channel metadata;
- valid and unique blendshape descriptors;
- valid and unique facial-bone descriptors;
- exact metadata/clip parity;
- absence of reserved morphs and forbidden gaze Transform paths.

Existing morph-only assets and schema-1 Pose compatibility remain valid.

## Automated coverage

Add focused tests using synthetic canonical figures and Unity test rigs; do not depend on the private DAZ library.

Importer/editor coverage must prove:

- an active `upperFaceRig` descendant rotation is retained;
- an active `lowerJaw` rotation is retained;
- an active lower-face descendant translation is retained;
- descendants without their own active source channels are not emitted merely because an ancestor moves them;
- `head`, `lEye`, `rEye`, neck, and body channels are ignored;
- similarly named bones outside the proven facial subtrees are ignored;
- a missing or ambiguous retained facial bone fails conversion;
- mixed morph + facial-bone Expressions contain both kinds of curves and descriptors;
- bone-only Expressions are valid and nonempty;
- sanitation leaving no morph or facial-bone channel fails;
- nonneutral facial scale produces the chosen explicit unsupported failure unless scale is implemented;
- clip/wrapper parity catches missing, extra, incomplete, and duplicate Transform curves;
- regeneration preserves clip and wrapper GUIDs;
- Pose generation remains unchanged.

Runtime coverage must prove for both position and rotation:

- bypass returns the exact incoming stream;
- zero, partial, and full intensity;
- incoming Pose changes remain live under partial intensity;
- `ClearExpression` reveals the current incoming Pose value;
- A→B and interrupted A→B→C transitions are continuous;
- disjoint facial-bone ownership releases A-only properties while applying B-only properties;
- repeated interruption does not leak stale contributions;
- Expression never changes `head`, `lEye`, or `rEye`;
- gaze acquisition, attention-life state, and timers remain independent;
- Blink closes fully over Expression-authored eyelid bone motion and reveals it after opening;
- disable/re-enable restores the last desired Expression coherently and does not resurrect waiters;
- native arrays/handles and playables have no lifecycle leaks.

## Manual acceptance

Use at least one real imported preset whose visible result depends on facial bones, and one mixed bone/morph preset.

For each, verify:

1. the generated report identifies the retained facial bone IDs and properties;
2. the `.anim` contains only the expected blendshape and facial-bone Transform curves;
3. jaw/brow/lip/eyelid articulation visibly matches DAZ at 100% intensity;
4. approximately 50% intensity blends from the current Pose-authored face;
5. switching and interruption are visually continuous;
6. Clear reveals the current Pose-authored face;
7. gaze continues to control head and eye orientation;
8. Blink closes fully and restores the Expression-modified eyelid state;
9. breathing phase and values remain uninterrupted;
10. a preset containing body or head pose channels does not transfer those channels into the Expression asset.

## Completion gate

This patch is complete only when:

1. facial-bone membership is selected by canonical hierarchy, not guessed names;
2. eligible active facial rotation/translation channels survive import;
3. nonfacial and gaze-owned Transform channels are absent;
4. wrapper metadata and clip curves have exact parity;
5. the runtime composes facial bones over the live incoming pose with continuous retargeting;
6. morph-only and bone-only Expressions both work;
7. existing Pose behavior and existing morph-only Expression assets remain compatible;
8. automated Unity/editor and runtime acceptance passes;
9. real DAZ bone-authored Expression presets pass manual visual acceptance.
